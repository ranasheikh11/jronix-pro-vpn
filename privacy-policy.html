Strict Zero-Logs Policy

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 30, 2026 | Last Updated: October 30, 2026
Summary Guarantee: Jronix Pro VPN is engineered with a strict Zero-Logs policy. We do not track, collect, record, log, store, or share your browsing history, destination IPs, DNS queries, downloaded data, or personal online traffic. What you do online is exclusively your business.

1. Introduction

Jronix ("we", "us", or "our") operates Jronix Pro VPN. This Privacy Policy informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and protection of information when you use our mobile application, desktop client, website, and WireGuard® tunneling services.

By using Jronix Pro VPN, you acknowledge and agree to the practices outlined in this policy.

2. Our Strict Zero-Logs Commitment

Our core mission is protecting user privacy. Our servers run in volatile RAM-disk environments where data is automatically discarded. Specifically, WE NEVER COLLECT OR LOG:

  • No Browsing History: The websites, apps, or services you access while connected.
  • No Traffic Destination or Metadata: The destination IP addresses or packet contents.
  • No DNS Queries: All DNS lookups are resolved privately through our encrypted zero-knowledge DNS resolvers.
  • No Original IP Addresses: We do not log your real public IP address connected to a VPN session.
  • No Connection Timestamps: We do not keep records of when sessions start or end.

3. Information We Process

To provide reliable connectivity and maintain our infrastructure, we only process minimal, non-identifying operational data:

  • Anonymous Device Identifier (UUID): An anonymously generated unique key stored locally on your device to maintain session states and server connection tokens. This identifier contains no personal name, phone number, email, or device hardware IMEI.
  • Aggregate Server Performance Metrics: Total aggregated bandwidth usage and server loads across all users (not tied to any individual) to ensure optimal server health, load balancing, and speed.
  • Optional Crash Reports: Anonymized diagnostic logs (stack traces without user data) if you explicitly opt-in to help fix application stability issues.

4. WireGuard® Protocol & Security Architecture

Jronix Pro VPN implements the cutting-edge WireGuard® protocol utilizing state-of-the-art cryptography:

  • ChaCha20-Poly1305: Military-grade authenticated symmetric encryption for all transit packets.
  • Curve25519: Elliptic curve key exchange providing Perfect Forward Secrecy (PFS).
  • BLAKE2s: High-speed cryptographic hashing for packet authentication.

Public keys are maintained strictly in volatile memory during active tunnels and wiped immediately upon disconnection.

5. Android VPNService Disclosure

In accordance with Amazon Appstore and Google Play Developer Policies, Jronix Pro VPN utilizes the native Android VpnService API. This API is essential to create a secure, encrypted network tunnel from your device to our high-speed servers. All device internet traffic passing through this interface is encrypted using the WireGuard protocol and is never inspected or logged.

6. Third-Party Services & Payment Processors

We do not sell, rent, or trade your data to third parties, advertisers, or data brokers. For in-app purchases, subscriptions, or downloads, transactions are processed directly by:

  • Amazon Appstore In-App Purchasing (IAP): Handled securely by Amazon according to the Amazon Privacy Notice.
  • Google Play In-App Billing: Handled securely by Google LLC.

We do not store or have access to your credit card numbers, bank accounts, or financial payment details.

7. Children's Privacy (18+ Policy)

Jronix Pro VPN is strictly intended for individuals who are at least 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect, solicit, or maintain personal information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the EEA/UK), in strict compliance with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Because we operate under a strict zero-logs policy, there is no personal browsing history or identifiable data retained on our servers. Any temporary session tokens or local settings can be immediately cleared at any time by uninstalling the application or clearing the application data in your device settings.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may periodically update this Privacy Policy to reflect improvements in our services, security upgrades, or regulatory requirements. Any modifications will be posted directly on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
